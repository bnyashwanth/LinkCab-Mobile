## 3. Common Issues & Solutions

### 3.1 Ride-Related Issues

#### **Driver Not Arriving**
1. **Wait 5 minutes** after scheduled pickup time
2. **Contact Driver** through in-app messaging
3. **Report Issue** if no response within 2 minutes
4. **Request New Driver** or cancel for refund

#### **Wrong Route Taken**
1. **Communicate** with driver immediately
2. **Use In-App Navigation** to show correct route
3. **Report Route Issue** after ride completion
4. **Request Fare Adjustment** if applicable

#### **Payment Issues**
1. **Check Payment Method** in wallet settings
2. **Verify Transaction** in ride history
3. **Contact Support** with ride details
4. **Allow 24-48 hours** for resolution

### 3.2 Account & Technical Issues

#### **Login Problems**
1. **Reset Password** using "Forgot Password" option
2. **Check Internet Connection** and app updates
3. **Clear App Cache** if login fails repeatedly
4. **Contact Support** with error screenshots

#### **App Crashes/Freezes**
1. **Update App** to latest version
2. **Restart Device** and clear cache
3. **Check Storage Space** on device
4. **Reinstall App** as last resort

#### **Location Issues**
1. **Enable Location Services** in device settings
2. **Grant App Permissions** for precise location
3. **Check GPS Signal** strength
4. **Move to Open Area** if indoors

## 4. Safety Guidelines

### 4.1 Before the Ride
- **Verify Driver:** Check photo, name, and vehicle details
- **Share Trip Details:** Send to trusted contacts
- **Wait in Safe Location:** Well-lit, public areas
- **Have Backup Plan:** Alternative transportation ready

### 4.2 During the Ride
- **Sit Behind Driver:** Safer seating position
- **Share Live Location:** Keep contacts informed
- **Stay Alert:** Avoid sleep or distractions
- **Trust Your Instincts:** Report suspicious behavior

### 4.3 After the Ride
- **Check Belongings:** Ensure nothing left behind
- **Rate Experience:** Help improve service quality
- **Report Issues:** Promptly report problems
- **Keep Receipts:** For payment disputes

## 5. Incident Reporting

### 5.1 Report Types
- **Safety Incidents:** Harassment, assault, threats
- **Vehicle Issues:** Breakdowns, accidents, damage
- **Service Problems:** Discrimination, route issues, overcharging
- **Lost Items:** Belongings left in vehicles

### 5.2 Reporting Process
1. **Access Incident Report** in app within 24 hours
2. **Provide Details:** Date, time, location, description
3. **Upload Evidence:** Photos, screenshots, recordings
4. **Submit Report** and receive confirmation number
5. **Track Status:** Monitor investigation progress

### 5.3 Follow-Up Actions
- **Police Reports:** Serious incidents require police filing
- **Medical Attention:** Seek immediate medical help if needed
- **Witness Statements:** Collect contact information if available
- **Documentation:** Keep all related documents

## 6. Refund & Compensation Policy

### 6.1 Refund Eligibility
- **Driver Cancellation:** Full refund if driver cancels
- **No-Show Driver:** Full refund after 10 minutes
- **Service Failure:** Partial/full refund based on issue
- **Technical Issues:** Refund for app-caused problems

### 6.2 Refund Process
1. **Submit Request** within 7 days of incident
2. **Provide Evidence:** Screenshots, ride details
3. **Review Period:** 2-3 business days
4. **Processing Time:** 5-7 business days to account
5. **Notification:** Email confirmation when processed

### 6.3 Compensation Types
- **Ride Credits:** For service failures
- **Discount Coupons:** For customer inconvenience
- **Wallet Balance:** Immediate refund to LinkCab wallet
- **Original Payment:** Refund to original payment method
