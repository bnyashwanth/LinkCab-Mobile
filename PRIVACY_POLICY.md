# Privacy Policy for LinkCab

**Last Updated:** February 13, 2026  
**Effective Date:** February 13, 2026

## 1. Introduction

LinkCab ("we," "our," or "us") operates a mobile ridesharing application that connects drivers with passengers for shared transportation services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

By using LinkCab, you agree to the collection, use, and sharing of your information as described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
- **Name and Contact Details:** Full name, email address, phone number
- **Authentication Information:** Profile picture, password (encrypted), biometric data (if enabled)
- **Location Data:** Current location, pickup/drop-off locations, GPS coordinates
- **Payment Information:** Wallet balance, payment methods, transaction history
- **Vehicle Information:** Vehicle type, license plate number (for drivers)
- **Usage Data:** Ride history, preferences, ratings and reviews

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Location Data:** Real-time GPS location during active rides
- **Usage Analytics:** App usage patterns, feature interactions, session duration
- **Performance Data:** Crash reports, performance metrics, error logs

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- **Payment Processors:** Stripe/Razorpay transaction data
- **Maps Services:** Google Maps location and routing data
- **Social Authentication:** Google/Facebook profile data (if used)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Service Provision
- **Ride Matching:** Connect passengers with drivers based on location and preferences
- **Route Optimization:** Calculate efficient routes using location data
- **Payment Processing:** Handle transactions and wallet management
- **Communication:** Facilitate contact between users

### 3.2 Safety and Security
- **Identity Verification:** Verify user identities to prevent fraud
- **Background Checks:** Screen drivers for safety (where applicable)
- **Emergency Services:** Provide emergency contact and location sharing
- **Incident Reporting:** Track and respond to safety incidents

### 3.3 Personalization
- **Route Suggestions:** Recommend frequently used locations
- **Driver Matching:** Suggest preferred drivers based on history
- **User Experience:** Customize app interface and features

### 3.4 Analytics and Improvement
- **Service Enhancement:** Analyze usage patterns to improve features
- **Performance Monitoring:** Track app performance and fix issues
- **Business Analytics:** Understand demand patterns and optimize services

## 4. Information Sharing

### 4.1 When We Share Information
- **With Other Users:** 
  - Driver name, photo, and rating to passengers
  - Passenger name and rating to drivers
  - Contact information for ride coordination
- **With Service Providers:**
  - Payment processors for transaction processing
  - Map services for location and routing
  - Emergency services when requested
- **Legal Requirements:**
  - Law enforcement when legally required
  - Regulatory authorities as mandated

### 4.2 When We Don't Share Information
We never sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Protection Measures
- **Encryption:** All data transmitted using SSL/TLS encryption
- **Secure Storage:** Personal information encrypted at rest
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access to sensitive data
- **Regular Audits:** Security assessments and penetration testing

### 5.2 Data Retention
- **Account Information:** Retained while account is active
- **Ride History:** Retained for 24 months for dispute resolution
- **Payment Records:** Retained for 7 years as required by law
- **Deleted Data:** Permanently deleted upon account deletion

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1 Access and Correction
- **View Information:** Access all personal data through the app
- **Correct Errors:** Update inaccurate or incomplete information
- **Data Export:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Account Deletion:** Permanently delete your account and data

### 6.2 Communication Preferences
- **Marketing Communications:** Opt-out of promotional messages
- **Transactional Messages:** Essential service communications cannot be disabled
- **Push Notifications:** Control notification preferences in app settings

### 6.3 Location Controls
- **Location Permission:** Grant or deny location access
- **Background Location:** Control background location tracking
- **Location History:** View and delete location history

## 7. Children's Privacy

LinkCab is not intended for children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 18, we will delete that information immediately.

## 8. Third-Party Services

### 8.1 Payment Processing
- **Stripe/Razorpay:** Secure payment processing
- **Data Shared:** Payment amount, method, and timestamp
- **Privacy Policy:** Subject to payment processor's privacy policy

### 8.2 Maps and Navigation
- **Google Maps:** Location services and route calculation
- **Data Shared:** Location coordinates and destination data
- **Privacy Policy:** Subject to Google's privacy policy

### 8.3 Social Authentication
- **Google/Facebook:** Account authentication services
- **Data Shared:** Name, email, and profile picture (with consent)
- **Control:** Only information necessary for authentication

## 9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in accordance with applicable data protection laws.
